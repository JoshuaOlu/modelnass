Who We Are

The team that builds the room.

The Model NASS Steering Committee operates under Care for Knowledge to design, run, and continuously improve the Model National Assembly experience. Each member leads a distinct function — from crafting the fictional world of Mona to managing partnerships and publishing research.

Together, they ensure that every session is rigorous, immersive, and worth a delegate's time.
