Session 01

The Boundary Proposal:
Who Owns the Future?

March 28 Emergency Session
Date March 28, 2026
Delegates 36 selected
Setting Federal Republic of Mona
Theme Territorial conflict · Resource rights

The inaugural Model NASS emergency session placed 36 delegates at the centre of the Tarsah-Ilen Plains crisis — a long-disputed border territory between Ugwulore State and Mineral Land State where the discovery of massive coltan deposits ignited violence, displacement, and a fractured national conversation about ancestry, faith, and sovereign wealth.

Delegates were called to draft a youth-driven policy resolution at the direct request of Mona's President. The session tested their ability to negotiate across identity lines, reconcile competing interests, and produce a resolution that could survive a divided chamber.

Themes explored
  • Territorial sovereignty
  • Natural resource governance
  • Identity and faith in policy
  • Emergency legislative procedure
  • Conflict mediation
  • Foreign investment ethics
